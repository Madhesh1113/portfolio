My Design Process

Every project follows a structured approach that keeps users at the centre of every decision.

01
🔍
Discover
User interviews, competitor analysis, and research to understand the problem space and the people we're designing for.
02
🎯
Define
Synthesise findings into clear problem statements, user personas, and measurable success criteria before touching Figma.
03
✏️
Design
Rapid wireframes → hi-fi mockups → interactive prototypes, with multiple iterations driven by feedback and data.
04
🚀
Deliver
Usability testing, developer hand-off with detailed specs, and post-launch measurement to validate impact.
